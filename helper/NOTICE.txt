Pointcast speech helper: what this folder is, and third-party notices
=====================================================================

whisper-server is the HTTP server example of whisper.cpp, built without changes from the whisper.cpp source release
named in Pointcast's manifest.json, by Pointcast's CI (https://github.com/naidx0/pointcast, workflow
.github/workflows/whisper-helper.yml; how to rebuild it is in helper/README.md there). Pointcast runs it on
127.0.0.1 to turn speech into text on this computer. The files here contain code from the projects below.

Accelerate are part of the system. Speech models are downloaded separately and carry their own licences.
