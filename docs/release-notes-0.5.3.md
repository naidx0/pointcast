**macOS** (Terminal): `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/naidx0/pointcast/main/install.sh | bash`
**Windows** (PowerShell): `irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/naidx0/pointcast/main/install.ps1 | iex`
Or download the file for your system below. Already on 0.5.x? Pointcast offers this update itself.

## Talk to it, and it talks back

- **Calls.** Press Ctrl+Shift+C and talk. Pointcast answers aloud, listens again, and you can talk over it to stop it. It no longer takes the end of its own voice, still coming back through your speakers, for you.
- **Read answers aloud** with your Mac's voices or the natural Kokoro voice (a 350 MB download, only when you choose it). Esc, the Ask key or talking over it stops it.
- **Voice wave:** a new **Bars** wave, a row of bars the voice lifts, next to Classic and Aurora (Settings › General › Appearance).

## It can use your computer, only when you let it

- **See and act.** Turn on the pointer switch in the Ask panel and the AI can look at and work in your apps. A small ring follows the pointer while it acts. Esc stops it.
- **You stay in charge.** Each app has its own level (look only, click, full). Anything that can't be undone waits for your **Do it**. A multi-step task shows a checklist with **Start** first. A journal under each answer lists what it did.
- **Files, browser, Mail and Calendar** are read through Pointcast's own tools: list, move and trash files (trash waits for you), read the front browser tab, newest Mail, upcoming events. Nothing is sent or changed without a click.

## Your AI, your model

- **Model and effort on every AI.** Claude, Codex and the agent apps each get their model and effort in Settings › AI, and Pointcast tells you when a different model answered than the one you picked.
- **More ways in:** Grok, Gemini, Groq, Mistral, DeepSeek, OpenRouter, any OpenAI-compatible address, or local Ollama and LM Studio, next to your ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini CLI and Copilot plans.

## Memory you can see

- **Memory file** on your computer: read it, edit it, add to it, clear it or switch it off (Settings › AI › Memory). A fact is kept only when you click to accept it.
- **The AI can say what it remembers.** It is told how memory works and shown your facts plus the title and gist of your last three sessions, so "what do you remember?" gets an honest answer.
- **One history on Home** for dictations and Ask sessions, with "Questions" and "Answers read" counted too. Reopening a long session starts from its summary and its last few turns. "Copy transcript" lets you carry a conversation to the AI's own app.

## Smaller things

- **Tooltips show right away** instead of after a second.
- The panel is easier to read: bigger text, bigger header buttons, a speaker and pointer switch, a New button, and a Copy button on a question.
- Window positions are remembered. The clock only shows when you hover.
- Updates keep your Screen Recording and Accessibility permissions once the app is signed with a stable certificate.
- **Windows** has not been compiled from this build yet; the installer and updater are as in 0.5.2.
