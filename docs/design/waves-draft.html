Voice wave drafts for the Ask panel

Four ways to draw your voice while you speak, in the panel's own size. Each one listens to the same voice, simulated or from your microphone.

A · Soft barsrecommended

Rounded bars that rise from the centre with your voice. A little randomness per bar, eased so it never jitters. When you're silent the bars settle into a quiet dotted rest.

B+ · Breathing wave, livelierchosen · what the Ask panel now draws

Each layer travels its own way at its own speed; the frequencies are unrelated and wander, and the swell drifts along the line, so it never settles into one spot or repeats. Faster and taller with your voice, still breathing at silence. Same maths as src/lib/line.ts (breathStep, breathAt).

B · Breathing wavefirst draft

Three soft mirrored waves layered, fading at both ends. No head, no tail. Closest to Siri.

C · Pulse dotsminimal

Five dots that grow with your voice in a ripple from the centre. The quietest option.

D · Today's line, cleaned upfor comparison

The current single stroke, without the red dot at its tip and with both ends faded, so it no longer reads as something swimming.
